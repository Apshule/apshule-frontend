Ap Shule LLP - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Location: Katabi, Wakiso, Uganda

1. INTRODUCTION

Ap Shule LLP ("AppShule," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, including the Education, Microfinance, Clinic/Pharmacy, and Agriculture modules. This Privacy Policy complies with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (Cap. 97) and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021 of the Republic of Uganda.

By using AppShule, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. DATA CONTROLLER AND CONTACT INFORMATION

  • Data Controller: Ap Shule LLP
  • Physical Address: Katabi, Wakiso, Uganda
  • Email: info@appshule.com
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): Nakaima Peninah
  • DPO Contact: dpo@appshule.com

3. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Education Module: Identity Data (Name, DOB, gender, NIN, student/teacher ID), Contact Data, Educational Data (grades, attendance, NCDC progress), Biometric Data (facial recognition for attendance), Technical Data.
  • Microfinance Module: Identity Data, Contact Data, Financial Information (income, loans, collateral), Biometric Data, Employment Data.
  • Clinic/Pharmacy Module: Identity Data, Contact Data, Health Status (medical history, allergies), Biometric Data, Prescription Data.
  • Payment Data: Financial Information (transaction amounts, history), Payment Method Data (MoMo numbers, transaction IDs).

4. SPECIAL PERSONAL DATA (SENSITIVE DATA)

Under Ugandan law, special personal data requires a higher level of protection. AppShule collects Biometric Data, Health Status, and Financial Information. We process special personal data only with your explicit consent and in accordance with section 9 of the Data Protection and Privacy Act.

5. PURPOSE OF DATA COLLECTION

We collect data for: Service Provision, User Authentication, Facial Recognition (with consent), Payment Processing (via Yo! Payments), Communication, Compliance (UMRA, NCDC, MoES), and Service Improvement.

6. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

We process data based on: Consent, Contractual Necessity, Legal Obligation, and Legitimate Interest.

7. DATA MINIMISATION PRINCIPLE

We comply with Section 14 of the Act. We only collect personal data that is necessary or relevant for the specified purposes. We do not process personal data which is in excess of what is authorized or required.

8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

You have the Right to Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restrict Processing, Object, and Data Portability. To exercise these rights, contact our DPO at: dpo@appshule.com.

9. DATA RETENTION

  • Student Records: 5 years after graduation
  • Loan Records: 7 years after loan closure
  • Medical Records: 7 years after last visit
  • Facial Recognition Data: Duration of active account
  • Payment Data: 7 years
  • User Accounts: Until deletion or 3 years inactivity.

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

10. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS

AppShule may transfer data outside Uganda with your consent, where the recipient country has adequate protection, or where necessary for a contract. We maintain records of all cross-border transfers.

11. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

We use Encryption (in transit and at rest), Role-based Access Control, Strong Authentication, Regular Monitoring, and Employee Training.

12. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the event of a breach, we will notify affected data subjects and the PDPO without undue delay, and document the breach (retained for 5 years).

13. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We share data with: Yo! Payments (payment processing), Hosting/Cloud Providers (infrastructure), and Regulatory Authorities (compliance). We do not sell your data.

14. CHILDREN'S DATA

We collect data from students with parental/guardian consent. Schools are responsible for obtaining this consent before registering students.

15. COMPLAINTS

Contact our DPO: dpo@appshule.com, or lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO).
